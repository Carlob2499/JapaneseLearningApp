# Photo provenance ledger (D-026)

Every photograph shipped in the app is licensed for redistribution and recorded here — the same
provenance culture D-002 mandates for language content, applied to imagery. Photos are downloaded
from Wikimedia Commons at build-asset time, cropped/compressed to WebP (sharp, ~1400px), and
bundled + precached; nothing is hotlinked at runtime. In-app treatment (the aizome duotone wash,
time-of-day tints, Ken Burns drift) is presentation only — the underlying works are credited below.
